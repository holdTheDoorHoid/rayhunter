About this dataset

Who runs it. [Your name or group], reachable at you@example.org. This is a community project. It is not run by the Electronic Frontier Foundation, and EFF did not review it.

What is here. Recordings that Rayhunter owners chose to send from their own devices. Before a recording leaves a device, the device's own identifiers (IMSI, IMEI, temporary identity) are set to zero in the capture, the owner's home network and WiFi details are removed, and the location is rounded, by default to about ten kilometres. Every entry was looked at by a person before it appeared.

What we receive but do not publish. Some owners choose to send the raw recording as well. It arrives encrypted to a key that is not on this server and is never published. It is decrypted only by a reviewer, on a separate machine, to understand a capture.

What we do not keep. Client addresses are not written to disk, by the service or by the web server in front of it. Submissions that never finish uploading are deleted after a day.

Withdrawal. The device that sent a recording can withdraw it at any time from its own settings page; the data is deleted at once and the entry is dropped from the next publication. To ask for removal any other way, write to the address above with the entry's id.

What a warning means. A warning is a pattern consistent with an attack on a phone's connection, not proof of one, and nothing here identifies who operated any equipment. See the Rayhunter documentation.

Reuse. The files here are published for research and reporting. Please cite the entry id and this site.
